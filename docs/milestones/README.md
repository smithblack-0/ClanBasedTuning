# Milestone gates

A gate states the cumulative result and evidence required to close one roadmap milestone. It does not choose implementation structure or act as a work tracker.

The governing milestone sequence and future outcomes live in [`../product_roadmap.md`](../product_roadmap.md). Accepted cross-milestone decisions live in [`../decisions/project_decisions.md`](../decisions/project_decisions.md).

## Active gate

- [Milestone 3 — integratable orchestration](gates/milestone_3_integratable_orchestration.md)

Only the active gate should contain detailed current acceptance requirements.

## Future gates

- [Milestone 4 — usability](gates/milestone_4_usability.md)
- [Milestone 5 — optimizer utility](gates/milestone_5_optimizer_utility.md)
- [Milestone 6 — industry readiness](gates/milestone_6_industry.md)

Future gates remain subordinate to the roadmap and must not predesign implementation that the active milestone has not reached.

## Completed history

Milestone 1 and Milestone 2 gates and plans are preserved under [`../archive/`](../archive/) and are not active authority.

A milestone closes only after human review confirms its required capability and evidence. Artifact existence alone is not completion.
